Questions

Frequently asked questions

What is an all-in-one business platform?

An all-in-one business platform is a single system that handles every digital part of running a business — the website, online bookings, payments, the domain name, and hosting — instead of stitching together separate tools for each. Tablecloth is an all-in-one business platform built for service businesses like salons, restaurants, clinics, and food carts.

How is Tablecloth different from Wix or Squarespace?

Wix and Squarespace are primarily website builders that add booking and payments through paid apps or higher tiers. Tablecloth treats scheduling, payments, staff management, and deployment as core parts of the platform rather than add-ons — so bookings, staff schedules, and your published site all read from the same database, with no syncing and no plugins to maintain.

Can I take appointments and payments on my own domain?

Yes. Tablecloth registers your domain, points it at your site automatically, provisions HTTPS, and serves the booking and checkout flow on your own domain. Customers never get redirected to a third-party booking subdomain, which keeps the experience — and the trust — yours.

Do my staff get their own logins?

Yes. Role-based access is built in. Owners see everything, managers run the schedule and the team, and technicians see only their own appointments. Permissions are enforced on the server, so a role can't be bypassed by poking at the interface, and access can be revoked instantly.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. Business owners edit their site, services, prices, hours, and photos from a visual dashboard, and publishing updates the live site immediately. Developers who want lower-level control get Git-connected deploys and full code access on the same platform — so a business can start simple and grow into custom work without migrating.

What kinds of businesses is Tablecloth built for?

Tablecloth is built for appointment-based and order-based local businesses: nail and hair salons, spas, barbershops, clinics, restaurants, cafés, and food carts. Templates cover service menus with durations and add-ons, food menus with online ordering, and retail catalogs.

Can I move my existing website over?

Yes. Tablecloth can rebuild an existing site on a modern template while keeping your branding, then transfer or re-point your current domain so there's no downtime and no lost search ranking.
