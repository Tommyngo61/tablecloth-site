Why this exists

Getting a good website usually means picking a bad trade-off.

Most small businesses end up choosing between doing it all themselves, or paying someone once and being left to run it. Neither ends with a site that stays good.

Do it yourself

Wix, Squarespace, Facebook page

Cheap to start, but you're the designer, the copywriter, and the support desk. Booking and payments cost extra on top, and the site quietly goes stale because nobody has time to update it.

Hire it out once

Freelancer or agency

You get something custom, then it's handed over. Hosting, the domain, and booking software are suddenly your problem, and every small change is another invoice — so changes stop happening.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Do I have to build the website myself?

No. We design and build the site for you. You describe your business, send over your services and photos, and we handle the design, the build, the domain, and the launch. There's no page builder for you to learn and nothing to drag around.

What does the monthly price cover?

Everything the site needs to stay online and working: hosting, SSL, your domain, the online booking system, software updates, security, and content changes you ask for. There's no separate hosting bill, no booking app subscription, and no per-appointment fee.

How is this different from Wix or Squarespace?

Those are tools you use to build your own site from a template, and booking and payments cost extra on top. Here you're not building anything — you get a site designed specifically for your business, with booking and payments already working, and someone who keeps maintaining it after launch.

How is this different from hiring a freelance web designer?

A freelancer typically builds the site, hands it over, and invoices hourly for changes — leaving you to manage hosting, the domain, and any booking software separately. We keep running the site after launch on our own platform, so hosting, the domain, booking, payments, and ongoing changes stay in one place under one bill.

Can customers book appointments on my website?

Yes. Online booking is built into every site, with real availability based on each staff member's schedule, service durations, and optional add-ons that extend the appointment. Customers book on your own domain and never get bounced to a third-party booking page.

Can I manage bookings and change my own prices?

Yes — that's what the dashboard is for. You can see today, this week or the month, filter to one technician, search a customer by name, add a walk-in, move or cancel an appointment, set your staff's weekly hours, and edit your services, prices, hours and photos. Publishing a change updates the live website immediately. It's all one login, on your phone or a laptop.

Will my salon show up on Google?

Your services, prices, opening hours and address are published in the structured format search engines read, so Google can show your salon with its hours and its service menu rather than just a link. Pages are server-rendered and built to load fast on a phone, which is what Google measures. Nobody can promise you a ranking — what we can do is make sure there's nothing in the way, and keep the site online so it stays indexed.

Can I run this from the iPad at my front desk?

Yes — it's designed for it. There's nothing to install: you open it in Safari and sign in. Buttons and time slots are sized for fingers rather than a mouse, the day's appointments fit a phone screen, and tapping a field doesn't zoom the whole page the way most admin tools do on an iPad. Your technicians can sign in on their own phones and see their own column.

Isn't it cheaper to just do it on Wix myself?

In money, yes — a Wix Core plan with bookings is about $29 a month plus your domain, and we're not going to pretend otherwise. What it costs instead is your time: you design it, you write it, you take the photos, and you're the one updating the holiday hours at 11pm. Where we're clearly cheaper is against the other route — a custom-built site with a proper CMS, hosting and a booking app runs $117–177 a month before anybody makes a single change for you.

Can my staff see their own appointments?

Yes. Every employee can have their own login. Owners see the whole business, managers run the schedule and the team, and technicians see only their own appointments — so nobody's looking at more than they need to.

How long does it take to launch?

Most sites go live about a week after we have your services, prices, hours, and photos. The build is fast because the platform already handles booking, payments, hosting, and domains — so the time goes into design and your content instead of plumbing.

Who owns the website and the domain?

You do. The domain is registered in your business's name and the content is yours. If you ever decide to leave, you keep your domain and your content.

I already have a website. Can you replace it?

Yes, and it's common. We rebuild it on a modern design while keeping your branding, then re-point your existing domain when the new site is ready — so there's no downtime and no lost search ranking.
